Overview

NCTU+ is a course and advisor review platform for NYCU students. Users publish structured reviews covering course content, workload, grading, and final grade, then search them by course, instructor, or tag before enrolling. The system is a React.js frontend backed by a Go API and MySQL, deployed with Docker.

100+ students have published reviews on the platform.

Features

Course reviews

Structured reviews with department, instructor, workload and grading ratings, and final grade.

Advisor reviews

A separate section for experiences with thesis and project advisors.

Keyword search

Search across all reviews by course name, instructor, or keyword.

Anonymous posting

Students can post reviews anonymously and browse them with instructor and course tags.
